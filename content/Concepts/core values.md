**Core values** represent the foundational beliefs that define what is important to you in life. identifying these values allows you to create a personalized code to live by, *ensuring your actions are driven by intention rather than reaction*.

**Key aspects of establishing core values:**

- **Defining the Code:** You must clearly identify what you are truly about and what matters most to you.
- **Ritual and Action:** These values are not just abstract ideas; they require **regular rituals** that connect you to them in your daily life.
- **Foundation for Depth:** The video argues that focusing on values is more effective _after_ building a foundation of discipline and capability, making your life choices more meaningful.
