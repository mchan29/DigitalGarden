- Noun
- **NOH-uh-sfeer** (/ˈnoʊəˌsfɪər/)
- the sphere of human consciousness and mental activity especially in regard to its influence on the biosphere and in relation to evolution

The term **Noosphere** (sometimes spelled _Noösphere_) refers to the "**sphere of human thought**." It is derived from the Greek word _noos_, meaning "mind."
