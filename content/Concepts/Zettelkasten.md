The **Zettelkasten** (German for "slip box") is a powerful method for knowledge management and note-taking that focuses on the **connections** between ideas rather than just collecting them

## The Three Types of Notes

|**Note Type**|**Purpose**|
|---|---|
|**Fleeting Notes**|Quick reminders or thoughts captured on the fly. These are processed and deleted within a day or two.|
|**Literature Notes**|Summaries of things you read or watch, written in your own words. Always include the bibliographic reference.|
|**Permanent Notes**|The final stage. These are the atomic ideas derived from your literature notes, fully integrated and linked into your slip box.|
