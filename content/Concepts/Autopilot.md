The brain uses a three-part cycle to move a task into autopilot:

1. **The Cue (Trigger):** A specific time, place, or emotional state.

2. **The Routine (Action):** The habit itself.

3. **The Reward:** The feeling of accomplishment or relief afterward.

When this loop repeats enough times, the **Basal Ganglia** (the part of your brain responsible for "autopilot") takes over from the **Prefrontal Cortex** (the part responsible for "willpower").
