The master clock is a small group of about 20,000 nerve cells in the brain called the [[suprachiasmatic nucleus (SCN)]]


1. **Sleep-Wake Cycles**: It determines when you feel alert versus sleepy by communicating with the [[pineal gland]].
2. **Body Temperature**: It regulates the slight dip in core temperature at night and the rise in the morning.
3. **Hormone Release**: Beyond cortisol and melatonin, it influences the timing of growth hormones and metabolic processes.
