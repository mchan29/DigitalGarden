

# Estimation and approximation errors (ERM)

what is [[Estimation and approximation errors (ERM)]]?


Preposition 

Proposition 4.1 For any sample S, the following inequality holds for the hypothesis returned by ERM:

$\Large \mathbb{P}\left[R\left(h_S^{\text{ERM}}\right) - \inf_{h \in \mathcal{H}} R(h) > \epsilon\right] \le \mathbb{P}\left[\sup_{h \in \mathcal{H}} \left| R(h) - \widehat{R}_S(h) \right| > \frac{\epsilon}{2}\right]$
