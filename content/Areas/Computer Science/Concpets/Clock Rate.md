What is a Ghz?
- he frequency at which a processor executes instructions, measured in hertz (Hz)

GHz = Gigahertz
- meaning a 3.5 GHz processor performs 3.5 billion cycles per second

Math to calculate Ghz? 

**1 Hertz (Hz)** = 1 cycle per second
**1 Megahertz (MHz)** = 1 million cycles per second
**1 Gigahertz (GHz)** = 1 billion cycles per second 

Higher Clock Rate = Faster Tasks
