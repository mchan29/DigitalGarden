**Neurodivergent** is an umbrella term for people whose brains process, learn, and behave differently than what is considered "typical."


Common examples include:

- **ADHD** (differences in attention and executive function)
    
- **Autism** (differences in social communication and sensory processing)
    
- **Dyslexia** (differences in language and reading)
    
- **Dyscalculia** (differences in processing numbers)
