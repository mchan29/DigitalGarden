1. **Seek wealth, not money or status.** Wealth is having assets that earn while you sleep. Money is how we transfer time and wealth, while status is your rank in the social hierarchy.
2. **Understand that ethical wealth creation is possible.** If you secretly despise wealth, it will elude you.
3. **Ignore people playing status games.** They gain status by attacking people playing wealth creation games.
4. **You're not going to get rich renting out your time.** You must own equity, a piece of a business, to gain your financial freedom.
5. **You will get rich by giving society what it wants, but does not yet know how to get.** You must do this at scale.
6. **Pick an industry where you can play long-term games with long-term people.**
7. **The internet has massively broadened the possible space of careers.**
8. **Play iterated games.** All the returns in life, whether in wealth, relationships, or knowledge, come from compound interest.
9. **Learn to sell, learn to build.** If you can do both, you will be unstoppable.
10. **Arm yourself with specific knowledge, accountability, and leverage.**
11. **Specific knowledge is knowledge you cannot be trained for.** If society could train you, it could train someone else and replace you.
12. **Specific knowledge is found by pursuing your genuine curiosity and passion** rather than whatever is hot right now.
13. **Building specific knowledge will feel like play to you but will look like work to others.**
14. **When you're accountable for your work, you'll get more leverage.** Embrace accountability and take business risks under your own name. Society will reward you with responsibility, equity, and leverage.
15. **Leverage is key to wealth.** Labor and capital have limits, but code and media are the ultimate leverage.
16. **You should be too busy to do coffee while still keeping an uncluttered calendar.**
17. **Set and enforce an aspirational hourly rate.** If fixing a problem will take less than your hourly rate, pay someone else to do it.
18. **Become the best in the world at what you do.** Keep redefining what you do until this is true.
19. **There are no get-rich-quick schemes.** That's just someone else getting rich off you.
20. **Apply judgment to leverage.** A well-judged decision with leverage can make you rich overnight.
21. **Be patient.** Become the kind of person who deserves wealth and wealth will find you.
22. **Play the long game.**
23. **When it comes to wealth creation, avoid competition.** The best entrepreneurs are not competing, they are creating.
24. **The ultimate goal is not just wealth, but freedom.** Freedom to do what you want, when you want, with whom you want, for as long as you want.
